About Miss'colkaz du soleil

Purebred Arabian Mare • 8 Years Old • 148 cm (~14.2 hh)

Offering for sale Miss'colkaz du soleil, a stunning 8-year-old Purebred Arabian mare measuring 148 cm (~14.2 hh). She features a very harmonious build, great charisma, refined breed type, top health metrics, and an exceptionally level-headed character.

Training & Qualifications

  • Endurance: Already successfully qualified over 22 km! Features an outstanding heart with extremely fast recovery/pulse rates and excellent natural stamina for endurance riding.
  • Hoof Status: Barefoot with strong, healthy hooves; moves completely soundly without shoes.

Temperament & Character

Extremely Level-Headed & Safe: A truly calm, bombproof, and confident mare that is afraid of nothing, bold, and very human-oriented. Incredibly easy to handle every day—grooming, barefoot trimmer care, tacking up, and riding are entirely stress-free.

Key Highlights & Suitability

  • Dressage & Conformation: Beautiful frame with refined features. Shows expressive movement, natural balance, and great presence.
  • Trail & Leisure Riding: Highly reliable outdoors, completely unbothered by traffic or unfamiliar environments.

Rider Profile

Ideal for teenagers, adults, or competitive riders looking for an easy, reliable horse in her prime with a fantastic brain and proven cardiovascular metrics—perfect to continue developing in endurance or as a high-end leisure partner.

Pedigree & Heritage

  • Breed: Purebred Arabian
  • Sire: Sen Misr – Known for passing down excellent breed type, stamina, and a calm, well-balanced temperament.
  • Dam: Colkazeenia du Soleil – From the renowned French Du Soleil performance line, famous for top-tier endurance genetics, toughness, and rapid recovery rates.
  • Grand Sire (Dam's Side): Coalfax S – Combining proven Polish and Egyptian lineages. Brings strong bone structure, exceptional hoof and tissue quality, and unwavering calm to the pedigree.

Genetics Summary: An ideal blend of French endurance performance genetics and established structural strength—the perfect recipe for stamina, heart-lung capacity, and mental soundness.

Health & Logistics

  • Up to date with all health care (vaccinations, deworming, dental, osteopath, barefoot trimmer).
  • Loads and travels smoothly in trailers or trucks; easy to tie up and groom anywhere.
  • Trials available on site. International transport (to Europe and beyond) can be easily arranged.
