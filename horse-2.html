About Lizzy de L'Urière

Purebred Arabian Mare (99.86%) • 5 Years Old • 148 cm (~14.2 hh)

Offering for sale Lizzy de L'Urière, a stunning 5-year-old Purebred Arabian mare (99.86% Pure Arabian) measuring 148 cm (~14.2 hh). She instantly catches the eye with her unique color and features a very harmonious build, excellent breed type, solid foundational training, and a remarkably calm demeanor. Born in France.

Training & Qualifications

  • Backed & Solid Foundations: Professionally backed with great care and understanding. Highly responsive to light aids with fantastic natural balance.

  • Hoof Status: Barefoot with strong, healthy hooves; moves completely soundly without shoes.

Temperament & Character

Extremely Level-Headed & Safe: A truly bombproof and confident mare that is afraid of nothing, bold, and loves human affection. Incredibly easy to handle every day—grooming, farrier care, tacking up, and riding are entirely stress-free.

Ideal for teenagers, adults, or competitive riders looking for an easy, well-backed young horse with an outstanding brain and head-turning looks—perfect to continue developing in endurance or as a high-end leisure partner.
