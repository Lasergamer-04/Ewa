About Holympede L'Urière

Anglo-Arab Mare • 9 Years Old • ~162 cm (16.0 hands)

Offering for sale Holympede L'Urière, a stunning 9-year-old Anglo-Arab mare (~162 cm / 16.0 hands). She displays an eye-catching, harmonious model, combining elegance, power, excellent dressage foundations, and an outstanding brain.

Qualifications

  • Showjumping: 1.15m courses.
  • Endurance: 3x 80km. One of the 80km races was the French Championship in Jullianges, where she finished in the top 8 individually.

Temperament & Character

Extremely level-headed and safe: A bombproof mare that is afraid of nothing, very confident, and loves human interaction. A true pleasure to handle and ride every day, both on the flat and on trails.

Key Highlights & Disciplines

  • Dressage & Conformation: Beautiful build with impressive natural balance, expressive movement, and light responsiveness to the aids.
  • Showjumping: Honest, generous, and reliable—jumping 1.15m courses with ease. Perfect for a rider looking to enjoy higher fences in total confidence.
  • Endurance: Qualified up to 3 x 80 km, ready to step up to the international stage (CEI*). She finished top 8 at the French championship in Jullianges. She boasts an athletic heart, excellent recovery rates, and great stamina for long-distance riding.

Rider Profile

Ideal for a rider seeking an immediate sport turn-key horse for medium-to-high showjumping, international endurance, or a versatile, top-quality sportpartner with an unbeatable temperament.

Pedigree & Prestigious Bloodlines

The backbone of a top-tier genetic cross, blending Arabian endurance and stamina with Thoroughbred scope, power, and athleticism:

  • Sire: Nabil Ben Maklouf (Purebred Arabian) – Direct descendant of the renowned Maklouf line, a chief sire line celebrated for producing top endurance horses with strong hearts, metabolic efficiency, and resilience.
  • Dam: Post Story (Thoroughbred) – Brings stride length, scope, energy, and the frame needed for jumping and speed.
  • Dam's Sire: American Post (Thoroughbred) – Exceptional stallion and multiple Group 1 winner (including the Poule d'Essai des Poulains / French 2000 Guineas and Prix Jean-Luc Lagardère). Known for passing down strong backs, charisma, easy temperaments, and great locomotion.

Genetics Summary: A highly sought-after Anglo-Arab cross giving stamina without over-reactivity, power over fences, and a high-performance cardio system.

Note: Holympe had a filly when she was 4 years old (Massilia de l'Urière, see last photo). Holympe is the full sister to my own mare Jalissa de l'Urière, who is currently performing in the Baltics.

Health & Logistics

  • Up to date with all care (vaccinations, deworming, dental, osteopath, hoof trimming).
  • Loads and travels effortlessly (trailer/truck); easy for shoeing and daily care.
  • Trials available on site. International transport (to Western/Northern Europe or beyond) can easily be arranged.
