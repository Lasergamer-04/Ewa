Orion des Aubus

About Orion

Purebred Arabian Gelding • 2023 • Chestnut

A beautifully bred young Arabian with an exceptional pedigree and exciting potential for endurance and sport.

Orion des Aubus is a 2023 purebred Arabian gelding, bred in France by Katell Lucas at Haras des Aubues. He combines some of the most sought-after Arabian bloodlines from Egyptian, Russian, Spanish and Polish breeding, creating a pedigree that is both elegant and performance-oriented.

Exceptional Pedigree

Orion is by FM Amir Al Baydaa, a son of the legendary Gazal Al Shaqab, one of the most influential Arabian stallions in modern Arabian breeding and a multiple World Champion.

Through Gazal Al Shaqab, Orion traces to Anaza El Farid and Kajora, while also carrying prestigious Straight Egyptian and Straight Israeli influences known for refined Arabian type, expression and presence.

His dam, Mytarabia des Aubues, brings a strong European and Russian performance influence. Her pedigree includes Mytic des Aubus, with lines tracing to Mystic Immage, Kubinec and Padron's Psyche, as well as the renowned Plakat family.

The maternal family also contains horses with documented endurance and sport performance, making Orion's pedigree particularly interesting for anyone looking for a future performance Arabian.

A Sport Prospect with Versatility

Orion's pedigree offers a particularly attractive combination of Arabian type, athleticism and performance heritage.

His bloodlines include horses associated with:

  • 🏆 Endurance
  • 🐎 Sport and ridden disciplines
  • ✨ Model & Allures / halter
  • 💪 Strong European, Russian and Polish performance lines

With his Arabian heritage and athletic pedigree, Orion has the potential to develop into a serious endurance prospect, while also being suitable for a variety of other sport disciplines.
